Sources and method

Numbered passages were checked against the sources below. Unnumbered passages repeat what the general literature says and were not individually verified, so treat their dates as the commonly given ones. Dates of invention, first use and commercial sale are kept apart wherever they differ.

No photographs or archival documents are reproduced. Watches marked “illustrative reconstruction” are drawings of a type, made for this page, and do not depict a specific surviving object. Watches from the archive are modern interpretive drawings made from published specifications.
